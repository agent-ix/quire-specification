the claim-form table, the two new table rows, the defining paragraph after
the table, and FR-290-AC-5. The FR-290 requirement statement, which carries
the SHALL, is unchanged. The new prose is definitional, and the table rows

Clean. Each new row states one claim form and one required kind. The
paragraph after the table defines one term ("scalar operation application")
and the claim-counting rule. FR-290-AC-5 gains one clause with one oracle:
