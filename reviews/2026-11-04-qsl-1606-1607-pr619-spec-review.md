## Verdict

The QSL-1606 clauses are well formed and say what the FR prose says. The FR-299 clauses encode two of the four old prose rules: no duplicate source obligation, and unrepresented or refused records claim no region. The prose keeps the other two. FR-269 has the `unrepresented` and `refused` members, and FR-298 has `source_obligation`, `disposition` and `output_regions`. FR-220's clause follows FR-227's "authored control node in the causal control graph": `OwnedChoice.occurrence` is a ProtocolNodeOccurrence. The three new properties match the Members table and are not added to the key. The `forall`, `filter`, `size`, `contains`, `implies` and `Type::member` forms are the ones the shared skeleton and the QSL corpus use. No AC changed.

The typed rows checked are justified by their own doc text or the FRs they cite. Every FR-287 key cites "one exact FR-287 of kind `<k>`" from its own Identity section. FR-249 sourceResultRef is the FR-286 `resultKey` `{producer, request, ordinal}` (FR-062). FR-287 authority_local_object_identity is "exact non-empty bytes" (FR-035), so Bytes is right. FR-252 period is FR-205 ExactRationalValue. FR-256 named_surrounding_execution is TemporalDecisionScope, consistent with named_decision_scope already being that type. FR-331, FR-410, FR-412 and FR-413 match schema.json `$defs`. Every new type name resolves to an object file. No property was renamed or removed. The reverted rows stay reverted.

**CONDITIONAL:** mergeable once FND-001 and FND-002 are fixed. FND-003 to FND-006 are small edits that can go in the same round.
