## Failure scenarios

- **FND-001.** An implementer follows `definitions/README.md` to
  `temporal-common.md` and implements "otherwise its future remains pending" with
  no decisive settlement basis and no activation-unknown state. That contradicts
  FR-093-AC-5 and FR-094-AC-7 at the same revision, and reproduces exactly the
  defect `spec/reviews/native-temporal-current/failure-domain.md` FND-001/002
  record as resolved. The corrected text exists only in
  `temporal-common-1-draft-3.md`, which the README disclaims as historical and
  tells consumers not to use as a selector.
- **FND-002.** A consumer resolving `protocol-finite.md`'s dependency table looks
  for `quire.state.graph/v1` at `1-draft.1`, digest `1d5ae5e6…`. No file in the
  branch has that revision or that digest; `state-graph.md` is `1-draft.2`,
  digest `8eb33fa2…`. The selector cannot be satisfied, and the protocol result
  contract the reviews corrected lives in `protocol-finite-1-draft-4.md`.
- **FND-003.** A reader treats the pinned FR digests as an integration gate,
  recomputes them, and gets 12 mismatches — with no way to tell an intentionally
  stale historical record from a real drift signal.

## Disposition

FND-001 and FND-002 are naming/selection defects, not semantic ones: the
corrected content is present in the branch. The cheapest fix consistent with the
"one current draft" directive is to make the unsuffixed file the newest content
for each family (promoting the `-1-draft-N` text and its revision label), then
delete the superseded copies and rely on Git history, as AGENTS.md now directs.
