## Summary

FR-322-AC-132 asked a schema to reject an owner pair absent from `model_selections`; a schema cannot join a node to a lock row (and schema.json never had such a rule or the dangling `ModelRootOwnerRule` STD-500 names: it is already gone on main). Moving the join and completeness to the reader matches the FR-322 owner-join paragraph, TC-310 MR-04 and IR #599. The edit does not weaken an obligation: the old rule was unsatisfiable by a schema and unenforced; the replacement keeps both halves (owner joins a row; each row has a root) as reader refusals with code, cause and pointer, and the closed shape stays schema. Positive control (seven fixtures admitted, schema and mirror reader) and negative controls MR-01..03 (schema) and MR-04, MR-06 (reader, test-local mirror) exist and are non-vacuous.

"The reader does not verify the region" is acceptable at the wire (no reader verifies any region against source text; the producer asserts it) but the text overclaims the oracle: FND-004.
