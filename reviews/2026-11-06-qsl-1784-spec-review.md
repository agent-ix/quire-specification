# Spec review of QSL-1784: carried quantity sum

## Summary

The commit adds `quire.op.collection.sum.carried_quantity`, the `sum<N>` whose `N` is a carried type `Quantity<u, C>`. It adds the checker and evaluator rules (FR-142 "Carried quantity sum", FR-142-AC-33/34), an empty-domain rule for FR-145 step 1, the wire reader rules (FR-322 "Carried-quantity sum", FR-322-AC-389), a per-family charge row in the value-accounting collection sum schedule, the schema identity, and the vectors TC-187 U45-U50 and CS-01-CS-06.

The design agrees with the existing FR-142 "Carried quantity" section. The carried sum is the one exception to the rule that a carried quantity is not an operand of quantity arithmetic. A summand must have exactly the type `N`, and a summand of another carried type must be converted explicitly first. It also agrees with FR-140 coefficient bounds (`Decimal[-500, 500; 2, 2]` is `[-5.00, 5.00]`, so `6.00` is outside and `3.75` is inside) and with the FR-140 target scale `smax`. FR-149 bounded-rational membership checks the reduced numerator and denominator: `9/20` is outside because its denominator is above 12, `8/1` because its numerator is above 7, and `7/12` and `1/1` are inside.

I recomputed every charge number in the vectors from the value-accounting tables. One `L(i,d,e,o,w,r)` tuple is `integer_bits, decimal_digits, unit_edges, value_occurrences, work_units, result_units`. All of these hold:

- U46: each addition charges 5 work units, and the whole sum charges 14 work units and 3 result units. The `unit.rational-arithmetic` amount is `max(2+1, 1+1)+1 = 4` and `max(1+1, 2+1)+1 = 4`. `unit.target-domain` is `bits(1) = 1` and then `bits(4) = 3`. Under `work_units: 13` the sum is incomplete at `collection.result-retain` with consumed 13 and next charge 1. Under `integer_bits: 3` it is incomplete with consumed 2 and next charge 4.
- U47: the sum is undefined after `unit.target-domain`, having charged 6 work units and no result unit.
- U48: the sum charges 1 work unit and 1 result unit. Over `Int[1, 5]` it charges nothing.
- U49: the sum charges 8 work units and 2 result units. The arithmetic amount is `max(3+2, 3+3)+1 = 7`, and the target-domain amount is `sbits(15,2) = 11` with `sdigits = 4`.

The FR-322 refusal bullets follow `quire.native.diagnostics/v1` step 6: laws, then mode, then member, then arguments, then `operation-mode-type-mismatch`, then `operator-ineligible`. The new SHALL statements are EARS conformant. No hash, digest, pin or tracking record is introduced; "pins" in this diff means a type fixing its rounding mode.

Cross-repo dependency (not a finding here): the catalog entry for `quire.op.collection.sum.carried_quantity` in `contracts/checked-operation-catalog-v1.json` in `agent-ix/quire-verification-contracts` does not exist yet. FR-322-AC-17's closed-catalog agreement needs it before this identity can be read end to end.

The review found three medium and six low findings. The main ones: (1) the mode-type rule for this identity uses `Q`, the `type_argument` node, as a source of the pinned mode, but the general FR-322 pinning rule does not; (2) several stated refusal paths have no vector; (3) the FR-145 step 1 change affects every `sum<N>` family but has no FR-145 criterion and no non-carried vector.

| FND-001 | medium | **Mode-type rule conflicts with the general pinning rule.** The bullet refuses "a `rounding` mode other than `Q`'s" with `operation-mode-type-mismatch`. That makes the `type_argument` `Q` a source of the pinned mode. The general rule (FR-322:3236-3240) and the native-diagnostics payload ("the set of mode values pinned by the operand and result types") take the pins only from the binder bound and the result type. Example: `type_argument` `Q` (`exact`), binder and result `Qn`, mode `nearest-even`. The general rule passes this, while the bullet refuses it with a payload whose pinned set equals the supplied mode. Separately, when the `type_argument` is not a carried node (CS-06), "`Q`'s rounding" is undefined at the mode-type step, which runs before `operator-ineligible`. Fix: keep the mode-type check to the general rule (mode against the pins of the binder bound and the result type). Classify a binder or result that disagrees with `Q` but agrees with the mode as `ill_typed`/`operator-ineligible` (`bound_is_member` / result not `Q`). ## Verdict

The semantics are sound and every recomputed charge number matches the value-accounting tables. FND-001 to FND-003 should be fixed before merge: one diagnostic-rule inconsistency, missing refusal vectors, and an FR-145 behaviour change with no criterion. The low findings are wording and traceability fixes. The QVC catalog entry is an open cross-repo dependency.
