# ADR-005: Ownership of Kani harness generation

## Status

**Open question for the architect.** Three specifications claim the same
generation step, with two harness surfaces.

## Context

[FR-196](../functional/ir-proof/FR-196-generate-kani-contracts.md) assigns
precondition derivation, harness generation and bound derivation to one
backend, and requires the harness to encode checked domain membership,
model-derived inclusive integer and collection bounds, frame conditions and
invariants.

`quire-contract-ir` places oracle, strategy, lowering and harness generation
inside its bounded-Kani boundary, while its own architecture decision assigns
deterministic provider generation, bounded Kani harnesses and replay adapters
to codegen. `ix://agent-ix/quire-contract-codegen/FR-003` and its
`interface-001` claim the same generation through `generate_kani_bundle`.

The two detail contracts also differ. The codegen `kani_slice` binding is one
Boolean input, one Boolean pre/post state and a customer
`fn(bool, bool) -> bool` path, carrying no precondition domain, frame or
invariant clause.

[subsystems/index.md](../subsystems/index.md) names S8's owner as the Contract
IR and codegen repositories together, so nothing arbitrates between them.

## Question

Which repository generates the Kani harness from a lowered contract package,
and what does that harness encode?

## Consequences

Until the question is answered, a proof obligation can be generated by two
subsystems producing different harnesses, and the
same subject can qualify under one and refuse under the other.
