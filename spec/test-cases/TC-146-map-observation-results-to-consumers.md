## Description

Planned consumer reconciliation controls for every acceptance criterion in
[FR-116](../functional/observation/FR-116-map-observation-results-to-consumers.md). No
executable test or completed coverage is claimed.

## Test Procedure

Deliver preserved, pending, incomplete, unsupported, conditional, unrepresented,
and refused per-obligation results to an executable/output consumer, including
open-subject decisive truth with its settlement basis and exact decision support.
Retain decision-scope progress, decision-scope closure, surrounding-execution
progress and surrounding-execution closure as four independently identified
facts. Vary compatible surrounding-execution progress and closure while retaining
the same decision-scope facts, settlement basis and exact deciding support.

For each independent mutation, hold any otherwise equal per-obligation truth
and target text constant. Independently omit, duplicate and cross-wire each of
the four required axes to another axis or an O2 scope in an O1 result, mutating
only the selected axis. Supply global-conformance claims with the fifth typed
closure record in each state (`closed`, `open`, `incomplete`, `contradicted`)
and the exact required execution, branch and workflow closure-premise identities.
Independently omit, duplicate, cross-wire or scope-mismatch that record and each
required global premise; substitute decision-scope or surrounding-execution
closure for the global record. Supply a non-global claim with `not-required`
and no global premise set. Independently add a global premise, replace a global
claim's record with `not-required`, and replace each other axis with
`not-required`.

Independently mutate assessment execution, each selected definition/profile
identity, revision and digest, and a late-result supersession link; attempt
Boolean-pass coercion. Compare structurally accepted target text with a
source-bound loss control, including a target unable to represent each required
axis or global premise independently. Independently omit every required source
assessment and mapping identity. Introduce a late contradiction retaining the
prior-result identity, contradicted premise and corrected input identity.

## Expected Results

Subject/capability/provenance and every revised result dimension survive. Mapping
loss remains classified, no non-success disposition is promoted to a pass,
definition/profile drift refuses, and structural target-text acceptance neither
changes the mapping outcome nor establishes semantic preservation. Earlier result
bytes remain associated only through a linked superseding or invalidating result.
An equal disposition with incomplete required provenance fails the mapping
reproducibility control.

All five progress/closure axes retain their independent identities. Compatible
surrounding-execution changes preserve independently settled truth, settlement
basis and exact support. Only a `closed` global record with every exact required
premise permits complete global success; the other global states remain visible
without erasing independently settled per-obligation truth. Non-global
`not-required` carries no global premises and cannot replace another axis.
Omitted, duplicated, cross-wired or scope-mismatched required axes, records or
premises and invalid `not-required` substitutions refuse the inconsistent input,
even with equal truth and target text. A target lacking a required representation
retains the source fact and explicit conditional, unrepresented or refused
mapping loss; it cannot report preservation.
