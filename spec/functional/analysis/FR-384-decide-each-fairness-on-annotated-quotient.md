# FR-384: Decide each-granularity fairness under symmetry on the annotated quotient

## Description

When a claim under symmetry carries a weak or strong fairness constraint of
`each` granularity, the engine SHALL decide each SCC of the reduced product
by the cycle group its edge permutations generate, so that the verdict equals
the unreduced verdict.

## Inputs

The retained reduced product of [FR-383](FR-383-explore-under-state-space-reduction.md),
whose edges are (source, transition identity, canonical target, permutation),
and the claim's fairness set.

## Outputs

For each SCC, whether it is fair for every `each` constraint, and for a fair
accepting SCC a concrete loop.

## Behavior

**Annotated quotient.** Along a walk of the retained graph, the concrete
state is `σ(canonical state)` with `σ` updated to `σ ∘ π⁻¹` at each edge. A
walk's **word** is the composition of the `π⁻¹` along it: at a node reached
with word `u` from a concrete entry `σ₀`, edge label `t` is the concrete
identity `σ₀(u(t))`.

**Cycle group.** For an SCC `C` with entry `c₀`, its first state in
discovery order, fix a spanning tree of `C` from `c₀` in canonical edge order
and let `u_c` be the tree word to node `c`. Each edge (`c`, `t`, `c'`, `π`) of
`C` gives the closed-walk word `u_c ∘ π⁻¹ ∘ u_c'⁻¹`. The cycle group `H_C` is
the group these words generate; tree edges give the identity.

**Concrete component.** For a concrete entry `σ₀(c₀)`, the concrete states
over `C` reachable from it are exactly `σ₀ h u_c (c)` for `h` in `H_C` and `c`
in `C`; they are mutually reachable, and the components for different `σ₀`
are images of one another under the run's group. Testing `C` once is exact,
including when the sort canonicaliser keeps duplicate representatives.

**Witness sets.** For an `each` constraint on operation `O`:
`W_taken = { u_c(t) : edge (c, t, …) of C with t of O }`,
`W_disabled = { u_c(d) : d an identity of O disabled at c, c in C }` and
`W_enabled` likewise with enabled identities, read from full enabled sets. An
identity `e` is taken, disabled or enabled somewhere in the concrete
component exactly when it lies in the `H_C`-orbit of the corresponding set.

**Weak `each`.** `C` is fair for a weak `each` constraint on `O` when the
`H_C`-orbits of `W_taken ∪ W_disabled` cover every identity of `O`.

**Strong `each`.** `C` is fair for a strong `each` constraint on `O` when
every identity of `O` in the `H_C`-orbit of `W_enabled` is in the `H_C`-orbit
of `W_taken`. The failing identities form a union of `H_C`-orbits, so the
strong-fairness refinement removes whole quotient states, those `c` where
`u_c(E(c))` meets them, and recurses on the SCCs of the rest, each with its
own cycle group.

**Concrete loop.** For a fair accepting SCC, the engine builds the loop from
the concrete entry by visiting obligations in canonical order (acceptance
sets, then `whole` constraints, then each `each` identity). For an `each`
identity `e` it finds a witness `x` at node `c` and `h` in `H_C` with
`σ₀ h u_c(x) = e`, writes `h` as a word in the generators by breadth-first
search over the generators in canonical edge order, walks the closed walk of
each generator in that word, then the tree path to `c`, then takes `x` (or
stays at `c` where `x` is disabled). It returns to `c₀` by the tree and
repeats the loop until the concrete state equals the concrete entry state.
Replay re-checks every `each` identity against the concrete loop.

**Cost.** Per SCC with `n` states and `m` edges, the spanning tree and words
cost `O(n + m)`, there are at most `m - n + 1` generators, and orbit closure
costs `O(I × g)` permutation applications for `I` identities and `g`
generators. Every step runs under the caller's budgets and meter, and
reaching one settles the item `incomplete`, FR-241 `resource-incomplete`, cause `limit-reached{limit, value, setting}`.

## Acceptance Criteria

| ID | Criteria | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FR-384-AC-1 | The cycle group of an SCC is generated by the closed-walk words of its edges over a spanning tree in canonical edge order; for the configs SCC with `va = 0` under the stabiliser of `a`, it is `{id, (b c)}`. | Test (TC-322) |
| FR-384-AC-2 | Under `fair weak each attemptUpdate`, the configs SCCs are rejected because the orbits of `W_taken ∪ W_disabled` miss `upd(a)`, and the instance settles `proved` with a reduced basis over 30 product states, the verdict the unreduced 135-state product gives. | Test (TC-322) |
| FR-384-AC-3 | For `eventually always holds(c.versionNumber != 2)` under `fair weak each attemptUpdate`, the 18-state SCC passes, and the concrete loop takes all three identities, passes `a = 2`, closes at the entry state and replays with each identity taken. | Test (TC-322) |
| FR-384-AC-4 | A strong `each` constraint removes every quotient state at which a failing identity is enabled and recurses on the remaining SCCs, each with its own cycle group, giving the unreduced verdict. | Test (TC-322) |
| FR-384-AC-5 | Reaching a budget during cycle-group or orbit computation settles the item `incomplete`, FR-241 `resource-incomplete`, cause `limit-reached{limit, value, setting}`. | Test (TC-322) |
